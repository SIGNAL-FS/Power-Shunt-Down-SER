⚠️ Caution and Preparation
  • Ensure minimum 2 manpower on standby for equipment verification.
  • Verify all spare parts (Fuses, CBTC PSU, Relays if applicable) are readily available before starting.
  • Always coordinate with Power Team (POW staff) before switching equipment.
Annex C: Steps for clearing of Non-Communicating Obstructions (NCOs)
Scenario 1 – UPS replacement work at station located at Zone Boundary
  1. SIG FS team to standby at both ZC stations of the zone boundary where UPS replacement work is carried out.
  2. IMCC shall perform remote reset of the MAUs of both ZCs concurrently. NCOs should clear upon successful restart.
  3. If the remote MAU reset command is unsuccessful or the MAU software hangs, SIG FS team shall perform a hard reset of the affected MAU(s).
  4. If any NCOs remain, OCC shall deploy sweep trains to sweep the affected tracks and clear the residual NCOs.
  5. If the MAU soft reset has been carried out, SIG FS will perform MAU hard reset after EOT.
Scenario 2 – UPS replacement work at a station not located at Zone Boundary
  1. SIG FS team shall standby at the ZC station where the UPS replacement work is carried out.
  2. IMCC shall perform a remote reset of the affected MAU.
  3. If the remote MAU reset command is unsuccessful or the MAU software hangs, SIG FS team shall perform a hard reset of the MAU. NCOs should clear thereafter.
  4. If the MAU soft reset has been carried out, SIG FS will perform MAU hard reset during EOT.
